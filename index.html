A few things to know before you begin.

How do I choose the right product?+

Your veterinarian is the best person to guide treatment choices, dosage, and administration. This catalogue helps you browse available formats and supplies; it does not replace veterinary advice.

What’s included in the starter kits?+

The reference catalogue lists an injection starter kit with seven days of supplies and a separate capsule starter kit. The full contents still need to be confirmed before ordering becomes available.

Can I place an order here?+

This is the first design preview. You can explore the collection and save items to your bag on this device. Payment, delivery, and order processing are not connected yet.

Where can I find the waiver and policies?+

The original site requires agreement to a waiver. The approved waiver, terms, and refund policy need to be supplied for Spring for Cats before checkout can launch.
