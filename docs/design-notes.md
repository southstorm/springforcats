# Storefront direction and current palette

Reviewed the four PDFs supplied by the owner: CureFIP home, injectable products, capsules, and FAQ/knowledge pages. They provide a useful visual benchmark: a solid green navigation bar, bold sans-serif headlines, direct shopping calls to action, prominent product/variant information, and distinct informational sections.

Spring for Cats adapts that visual confidence through the owner’s preferred green-led variant. Color proportions follow visual hierarchy rather than a fixed page-area ratio:

- A warm ivory (#F3F0EB) canvas, white (#FFFFFF) navigation/cards/forms, and slate (#1E293B) text with large sans-serif headlines.
- Its existing original cat photograph, now in a rectangular framed crop rather than an arch.
- Strong product/category cards, larger prices, clear ten-capsule pack labels, and full-width purchase buttons.
- A three-step guide to browsing formats, choosing packs, and building a bag.
- Larger FAQ cards and clear section contrasts, with navigation visible on phones.
- Brand green (#4A7C59) on the logo, selected large headings, secondary actions and supporting surfaces; a darker green (#3D684A) is used for small text on ivory where needed for contrast.
- Deep coral (#B94732) with white lettering for primary actions, and soft coral (#E76F51) for small decorative dots and dividers.
- White cards on ivory, soft sage promotions and success cues. Alerts have separate treatments: burgundy/pink errors, slate/blue informational notices and a green check-marked success message.

The branding, layout, copy, and generated cat photograph belong to this project's design. CureFIP's logos, packaging artwork, customer reviews, clinical outcome percentages, efficacy claims, and treatment protocols have not been imported. The original uploaded PDFs are not included in the public repository.

Product prices, stock behavior, order requests, optional promotion discounts, flavor variants, and ten-capsule pack requirements remain as configured for Spring for Cats. New default headline/copy is applied only to unchanged starter text; owner-customized text is retained in the database and offline demo.
